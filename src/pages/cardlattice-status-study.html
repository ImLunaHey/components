How progress is measured

Progress is the share of sets at each status. A set is not started if it has no printings, in progress if any known printing lacks a finish, illustrator or rarity, and done when every known printing has all three recorded.

A printing counts once even if it has multiple finish options. These numbers are illustrative, not a live snapshot.
